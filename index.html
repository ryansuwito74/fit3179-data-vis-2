Section 01

Prevalence & Demographic Vulnerability

Geographic Distribution of Mental Disorders

Regional Exposure: Regional and outer territories report the highest distress rates, bypassing the urbanised east coast.

Anxiety by Age Cohort & Gender

Young Demographics: Females aged 16–24 experience the highest relative anxiety burden, nearly doubling observed rates among older demographics.

Section 02

Long-Term Shifts & Socio-Economic Burden

10-Year Psychological Distress Drift

Steepest Trajectory: Severe distress rates among youth grew by over 12 percentage points over the decade, widening the gap across generations.

Psychological Distress Severity Across Age Cohorts

Severe Distress Concentration: Over 25% of young Australians aged 16–24 experience high or very high psychological distress, more than 2.5 times the rate observed in older cohorts.

Section 03

Geographic Inequity & Patient Mobility

Multi-Dimensional Clinical Profile by State

Multi-Axis Burden: Queensland records the highest concurrent rates of active 12-month disorders (23.7%), severe distress (18.9%), and lifetime suicidal ideation (21.1%) among major states.

Patient Volume & Severity Across Primary Health Networks

Metropolitan Volume: Major urban PHNs carry the highest raw patient volume, while regional and outer hubs record elevated severe disorder concentrations.

Medicare Service Uptake by Provider & Gender

The Gender Service Gap: Women access clinical psychology and GP mental health plans at nearly double the rate of men, with service uptake gaps exceeding 70 visits per 1,000 residents across primary care providers.

Evolution of Medicare Mental Health Consultations

Pandemic Surge: Annual consultations surged to 14 million sessions during the 2020–21 pandemic peak, driven by expanded GP mental health plans and psychology subsidies.

Section 04

System Capacity & Treatment Pathways

Spatial Distribution of SA4 Diagnostic Prevalence

Regional Concentration: Regional hubs exhibit heightened diagnostic rates exceeding 12% in areas like Ballarat (12.5%) and Newcastle (12.2%), contrasting with sub-6% rates across outer metropolitan corridors.

Demand Rank Shifts Across Age Cohorts

Cohort Trajectories: Young adults aged 15–24 persistently dominate primary consultation demand ranks, while middle-age cohort ranks have stabilized following pandemic spikes.

Clinical Diagnoses Breakdown by Disorder Category

Diagnostic Composition: Anxiety conditions represent the largest single diagnostic class, led by social phobia and generalized anxiety, followed closely by depressive episodes.

State Prevalence Against National Benchmarks

Target Variance: Tasmania and Queensland exceed the national threshold benchmark for high-distress prevalence, while Victoria and New South Wales track closely along moderate bands.
