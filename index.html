Section 01

Prevalence & Demographic Vulnerability

Establishing baseline national disorder distribution and examining pronounced demographic vulnerabilities across gender and age cohorts.

Geographic Distribution of Mental Disorders

12-month adult mental disorder prevalence across Australian States and Territories (ABS NHS baseline).

Regional Exposure: Northern Territory (28.8%) and Queensland (23.7%) record the highest active disorder rates, exceeding southern metropolitan baselines.

Anxiety by Age Cohort & Gender

Proportion reporting 12-month anxiety disorders, comparing ■ Female (left) and ■ Male (right) cohorts across lifecycle brackets.

Young Demographics: Young females aged 16–24 carry the highest relative anxiety burden (44.8%), nearly doubling rates observed among older demographics.

Section 02

Long-Term Shifts & Socio-Economic Burden

Analyzing ten-year psychological distress shifts and evaluating how distress severity concentrates across age cohorts.

10-Year Psychological Distress Drift

Prevalence change (2011 to 2021), tracking the steep escalation among ■ Youth (15–24) relative to adult cohorts.

Steepest Trajectory: Severe psychological distress among youth surged by +12.4 percentage points over the decade, widening cross-generational disparity.

Psychological Distress Severity Across Age Cohorts

Grouped bar comparison of K10 distress levels across lifecycle cohorts; high and very high distress disproportionately impacts younger Australians.

Distress Concentration: Over 25.8% of Australians aged 16–24 endure high or very high psychological distress, 2.5 times the rate observed in older cohorts.

Section 03

Geographic Inequity & Clinical Utilization

Investigating multi-dimensional psychiatric burden, Primary Health Network allocations, and structural gender gaps in Medicare uptake.

Multi-Dimensional Clinical Profile by State

Comparing 5 psychiatric indicators across jurisdictions; ■ Queensland persistently outpaces southern states on active psychiatric burden.

Cross-Metric Burden: Queensland exhibits concurrent peaks in active disorders (23.7%), severe distress (18.9%), and lifetime suicidal ideation (21.1%).

PHN Patient Volume & Disorder Severity

Bubble size denotes total patients diagnosed; color intensity denotes percentage of patients with severe mental illness.

Urban Volume vs Regional Severity: Metropolitan PHNs absorb the largest raw caseloads, while regional and remote networks report elevated severe case ratios.

Medicare Service Uptake by Provider & Gender

Per-capita consultation gap between ■ Female and ■ Male patients across primary mental health provider tiers.

The Gender Utilization Divide: Women access clinical psychology and GP plans at nearly double the rate of men, exceeding 70 visits per 1,000 residents in primary care.

Evolution of Medicare Mental Health Consultations

Stacked annual consultation volume from zero-baseline by clinician type, highlighting the 2020–21 Pandemic Surge (14.0M sessions).

Telehealth & GP Surge: Consultations peaked at 14 million sessions during COVID-19 lockdowns, driven by subsidized psychology and GP management items.

Section 04

System Capacity & Treatment Benchmarks

Evaluating sub-regional clinical demand, lifecycle consultation rank dynamics, clinical diagnostic composition, and state benchmark variance.

Spatial 2D Binned Grid of Regional Diagnostic Prevalence

Spatial aggregation into 1.5° grid cells. Stepped bins: ■ <8%, ■ 8–12%, and ■ >12% (Peak Cluster).

Regional Demand Hotspots: Spatial binning reveals concentrated diagnostic demand exceeding 12% across Central Victoria and the Hunter Valley, contrasting with lower outer-suburban cells.

Demand Rank Shifts Across Age Cohorts

Tracking annual demand ranks (1 = Highest); ■ Youth (18–24) surged to rank 1 during the 2020–22 pandemic shock.

Rank Dynamics: Young adults (18–24) overtook all older demographics in MBS service intensity by 2019–20, maintaining peak utilization through 2022.

Clinical Diagnoses Breakdown by Disorder Category

Diagnosed prevalence across ■ Anxiety, ■ Affective, and ■ Substance Use disorder families.

Diagnostic Composition: Anxiety conditions represent the largest clinical burden, led by social phobia (401.8k) and PTSD (295.8k), followed by depressive episodes.

State Prevalence Against National Benchmarks

State rates evaluated against qualitative distress bands and the ❙ National Target Benchmark (21.5%).

Target Variance: Northern Territory (28.8%) and Queensland (23.7%) substantially exceed the 21.5% national threshold, while Victoria (21.4%) aligns with targets.
